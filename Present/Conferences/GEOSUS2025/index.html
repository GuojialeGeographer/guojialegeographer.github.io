International Conference on Geography and Sustainability 2025 (GEOSUS 2025) 2025年地理学与可持续发展国际会议

As a transdisciplinary discipline studying the coupled human and nature systems and their interactions, geography has natural advantages to promote sustainable development by providing underpinning theories, methodologies, models and data. Geographers have made important contributions to exploring the nexus between the natural and social sciences, and delivered knowledge and experiences that are needed to support transformation towards sustainable development. The International Conference on Geography and Sustainability 2025 (GEOSUS 2025) aims to handle the challenges associated with geography and sustainable development by interdisciplinary research methods, strengthen exchanges and cooperation among different disciplines, and promote the integration and innovation of geography and sustainability. 作为一门研究人地耦合系统及其相互作用的跨学科领域，地理学在通过提供基础理论、方法、模型和数据来促进可持续发展方面具有天然优势。地理学家在探索自然科学与社会科学之间的联系方面做出了重要贡献，并提供了支持向可持续发展转型所需的知识和经验。2025年地理学与可持续发展国际会议（GEOSUS 2025）旨在通过跨学科研究方法应对地理学与可持续发展相关的挑战，加强不同学科之间的交流与合作，促进地理学与可持续发展的融合与创新。

Conference Theme 会议主题
Geography and Sustainable Development: From a Transdisciplinary Perspective 地理学与可持续发展：跨学科视角

The conference will explore how geography, as a transdisciplinary field, contributes to sustainable development through interdisciplinary research approaches, addressing the complex interactions between human and natural systems. 本次会议将探讨地理学作为跨学科领域如何通过跨学科研究方法为可持续发展做出贡献，应对人地系统之间复杂的相互作用。

Conference Objectives 会议目标
  • To address challenges associated with geography and sustainable development through interdisciplinary research methods
  • 通过跨学科研究方法应对地理学与可持续发展相关的挑战
  • To strengthen exchanges and cooperation among different disciplines
  • 加强不同学科之间的交流与合作
  • To promote the integration and innovation of geography and sustainability
  • 促进地理学与可持续发展的融合与创新
  • To explore the nexus between natural and social sciences in the context of sustainable development
  • 探索可持续发展背景下自然科学与社会科学之间的联系
  • To provide a platform for sharing knowledge and experiences in geography and sustainability research
  • 为分享地理学与可持续发展研究的知识和经验提供平台
