Notice on the 20th Annual Conference on Geographic Information Science Theory and Methods (Second Notice) 关于召开第二十届地理信息科学理论与方法学术年会的通知（第二号）

The 20th Annual Conference on Geographic Information Science Theory and Methods will be held in Xiamen from November 21-23, 2025. This conference is guided by the China Association for Geographic Information Society, jointly organized by the Committee on Geographic Information Science Theory and Methods of CAGIS and Jimei University, and hosted by the School of Computer Science of Jimei University and the School of Computer and Information Engineering of Xiamen University of Technology. The conference will focus on theoretical methods, technical applications, and industrialization of geographic information science. 第二十届地理信息科学理论与方法学术年会定于2025年11月21-23日在厦门举办。本次大会由中国地理信息产业协会指导，中国地理信息产业协会地理信息科学理论与方法工作委员会和集美大学联合主办，集美大学计算机学院和厦门理工学院计算机与信息工程学院承办。本次会议将紧扣地理信息科学理论方法、技术应用及产业化等主题展开研讨。

V. Call for Special Sessions and Papers 五、专题报告会征集及论文征稿
1. Call for Special Sessions: 1. 专题报告会征集：

Experts who intend to organize special sessions to promote team innovation achievements and major projects should propose the name of the special session and participating members (6-8 people), fill in the special session application form (available on the conference website, named as: Institution - Expert Name - Special Session Name.doc) and send it to the conference email: gis2025jmu@163.com before November 1, 2025.

有意向组织专题报告会宣传团队创新成果、重大项目等的专家，请拟定专题报告会名称及参与成员（6～8人），填写专题报告申请表（见会议网站，命名为：单位－专家姓名－专题报告会名称.doc）并于2025年11月1日前发送至大会邮箱：gis2025jmu@163.com。

When the number of reports in a special session is less than 6, the organizing committee will merge topics or add free submissions that match the topic. Each scholar should in principle only report in one special session.

当专题报告会报告数少于6个时，组委会将视情况合并专题，或添加与专题契合的自由投稿报告。每位学者原则上仅应在一个专题进行报告。

2. Call for Papers: 2. 论文征稿：

This conference will include oral presentations, flash presentations, and posters. Those who wish to submit should log in to the conference website and submit abstracts before November 1, 2025. There is no specific format requirement for abstracts, and the recommended length is 300-800 words.

本次会议将设置口头报告、快闪报告和海报，请有意投稿者于2025年11月1日前登录会议网站提交摘要。摘要格式不限，字数建议300-800字。

The conference will also set up a Young Scholar Competition session and a Student Paper Competition session to select award-winning reports. Participants in the competition need to submit full papers. Competition papers must be unpublished papers. For paper format requirements, please refer to the submission channel on the conference website.

同时，本次会议将设置青年学者竞赛分会场和学生论文竞赛分会场，评选获奖报告。参加竞赛的参会者需提交论文全文，参赛论文须为未公开发表过的论文，论文格式要求详见会议网站投稿通道。

Excellent reports from this conference will be invited by collaborating Chinese core journals to organize special columns for publication.

本次会议的优秀报告，将由会议协作中文核心期刊会后邀稿，组织专栏发表。
