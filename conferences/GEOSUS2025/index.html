International Workshop on Geography and Sustainability 2025 (GEOSUS 2025) 2025年地理学与可持续发展国际研讨会

As a transdisciplinary discipline studying the coupled human and nature systems and their interactions, geography has natural advantages to promote sustainable development by providing underpinning theories, methodologies, models and data. Geographers have made important contributions to exploring the nexus between the natural and social sciences, and delivered knowledge and experiences that are needed to support transformation towards sustainable development. The International Workshop on Geography and Sustainability 2025 (GEOSUS 2025) aims to handle the challenges associated with geography and sustainable development by interdisciplinary research methods, strengthen exchanges and cooperation among different disciplines, and promote the integration and innovation of geography and sustainability. We look forward to you joining us in Xi'an, China in October 2025! 作为一门研究人地耦合系统及其相互作用的跨学科领域，地理学在通过提供基础理论、方法、模型和数据来促进可持续发展方面具有天然优势。地理学家在探索自然科学与社会科学之间的联系方面做出了重要贡献，并提供了支持向可持续发展转型所需的知识和经验。2025年地理学与可持续发展国际研讨会（GEOSUS 2025）旨在通过跨学科研究方法应对地理学与可持续发展相关的挑战，加强不同学科之间的交流与合作，促进地理学与可持续发展的融合与创新。我们期待您于2025年10月加入我们在中国西安的会议！

I. Time and Location 一、时间地点
Time: 时间： October 17-20, 2025 2025年10月17-20日
Location: 地点： Xi'an, China 中国西安
II. Conference Theme 二、会议主题
Theme: 主题： Geography and Sustainable Development: From a Transdisciplinary Perspective 地理学与可持续发展：跨学科视角

The workshop will explore how geography, as a transdisciplinary field, contributes to sustainable development through interdisciplinary research approaches. We look forward to welcoming researchers, practitioners, and students from around the world to join us in Xi'an, China in October 2025! 本次研讨会将探讨地理学作为跨学科领域如何通过跨学科研究方法为可持续发展做出贡献。我们期待欢迎来自世界各地的研究人员、实践者和学生于2025年10月加入我们在中国西安的会议！

III. Conference Objectives 三、会议目标
  • To address challenges associated with geography and sustainable development through interdisciplinary research methods
  • 通过跨学科研究方法应对地理学与可持续发展相关的挑战
  • To strengthen exchanges and cooperation among different disciplines
  • 加强不同学科之间的交流与合作
  • To promote the integration and innovation of geography and sustainability
  • 促进地理学与可持续发展的融合与创新
  • To explore the nexus between natural and social sciences in the context of sustainable development
  • 探索可持续发展背景下自然科学与社会科学之间的联系
  • To provide a platform for sharing knowledge and experiences in geography and sustainability research
  • 为分享地理学与可持续发展研究的知识和经验提供平台
IV. Target Audience 四、目标受众

The workshop welcomes researchers, practitioners, graduate students, and professionals working in geography, environmental science, sustainability studies, urban planning, and related fields who are interested in exploring the intersection of geography and sustainable development. 本次研讨会欢迎从事地理学、环境科学、可持续发展研究、城市规划及相关领域的研究人员、实践者、研究生和专业人士参加，共同探讨地理学与可持续发展的交叉领域。
